2. Relational Database Schema (MySQL 8.0+ / 9.0+)

The database ev_market_db is structured as a normalized relational schema (Third Normal Form) in MySQL with InnoDB storage engine, foreign key cascade constraints, unique keys, and analytical indexes.

3. Data Harmonization Pipeline & Quality Audit

Rule 1: OEM Canonical Resolution

Brand variations (e.g. "Mercedes", "VW", "Skoda", "Citroen") standardized to official corporate entities ("Mercedes-Benz", "Volkswagen", "Škoda", "Citroën").

Rule 2: Battery Chemistry Taxonomy

Mapped unstructured battery labels to standardized electrochemical codes: LFP, NMC, NCA, Sodium-Ion, and Solid-State.

Rule 3: Automotive Category Mapping

Harmonized 15 raw body styles into 6 standardized categories: SUV, Sedan, Hatchback, Pickup, Van, and Sports Car.

Rule 4: Currency Normalization

Base prices converted from EUR to USD using 1.08 baseline exchange rate with battery-cost cross-validation.

4. Assumptions, Disclosures & Limitations

  • WLTP vs. EPA Range Normalization: Driving ranges reported under the EPA cycle were adjusted to WLTP equivalent using a 1.12x conversion factor where direct WLTP testing was pending.
  • Net Usable Battery Capacity: When net battery capacity was undocumented, it was modeled at 94% of gross pack capacity reflecting standard buffer configurations.
  • Customer Satisfaction Benchmarking: Consumer ratings are derived from aggregate manufacturer and category studies (J.D. Power EVX Ownership Study and Consumer Reports EV Reliability Index). They are explicitly modeled at the brand/category level rather than individual vehicle level.
  • Macro Sales vs. OEM Volume: Regional sales totals represent macro country registrations from the IEA, while manufacturer-specific sales reflect the global competitive set. Macro totals and OEM breakdowns are queried with mutually exclusive criteria in SQL to prevent double-counting.
